Booking

Slots are confirmed on advance booking over call or WhatsApp. Arrive 10 minutes early — play stops on time so the next team can start.

Footwear

Turf shoes or flat-soled sports shoes only. Metal studs, spikes and outdoor footwear are not allowed on the playing surface.

On the turf
