About Us

Built for the game, not the gallery.

BOXPARK is a unit of Karni Sports Club — a modern box-sports arena tucked into Panchyawala, Vaishali Nagar, Jaipur.

We built it for the people who actually play: the office team that meets at 10 PM, the under-15 who wants one more net, the group that turns up every Sunday without fail. Full-size caged turf, true bounce, LED floodlights that make midnight feel like noon, and enough parking that nobody circles the block.

Everything here is run by sportspeople. Our academies are led by nationally capped athletes, and the ground is maintained the way it should be — every single day.
